I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences in Leipzig, and a member of the <em>Stochastic Analysis in Mathematical Physics</em> group led by <a href='https://www.mis.mpg.de/people/nikolay-barashkov' target='_blank'>Nikolay Barashkov</a>.

From August to December 2025, I was on a leave at the Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute in Berkeley, where I participated as a postdoctoral member in the semester-long program <em>Recent Trends in Stochastic Partial Differential Equations</em>.

Previously, I completed my PhD in mathematical physics at Université Paris-Saclay, under the co-supervision of <a href='https://annuaire.in2p3.fr/6225-7630/vincent-rivasseau' target='_blank'>Vincent Rivasseau</a> and <a href='https://sites.google.com/view/ajaychandra/home' target='_blank'>Ajay Chandra</a>.

My research interests lie in the probabilistic and analytic aspects of quantum field theory, in particular the study of singular equations and measures, and the renormalisation group.
